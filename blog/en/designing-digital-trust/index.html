Designing digital trust: an introduction and a new challenge

calendar_today September 21, 2026
schedule 3 min read

My professional career has developed at the intersection of networks, technological architecture, and cybersecurity. For over fifteen years, I have worked on the design, implementation, and evolution of complex technological environments, especially in critical infrastructures, data centers, and hybrid cloud scenarios. This experience has allowed me to understand that security is not an isolated component: it is an indispensable condition for digital services to operate with trust and continuity.

From technical architecture to strategic vision

Throughout my career, I have participated in building network and cybersecurity architectures, as well as consolidating a security architecture function. Each of these challenges has reinforced a conviction: protecting an organization goes far beyond implementing technical controls. As environments grow and become more interdependent, security also demands governance, prioritization, and the ability to explain risks and investments in a language close to the business areas.

My background as a Communications and Electronics Engineer, along with my experience in technological architecture, has given me a technical foundation that I deeply value. However, the next step in my professional development requires complementing that perspective with knowledge that allows connecting cybersecurity with the continuity of production chains, the protection of information assets, and the quality of services that customers receive.

Why I am studying the MCY

For this reason, I decided to pursue a Master's in Cybersecurity at Tecnológico de Monterrey. My goal is to prepare myself to evolve towards the role of CISO, expanding my capabilities in risk management, leadership, and strategic communication. I seek to strengthen the ability to work with business leaders and present cybersecurity as an indispensable investment for resilience and sustainable growth, not solely as a response to incidents.

For me, this postgraduate degree represents the opportunity to integrate my technical experience with a more comprehensive vision of the organization. I aspire to contribute so that security decisions become enablers of trust: decisions that reduce exposure to risks, preserve operations, and accompany innovation.
